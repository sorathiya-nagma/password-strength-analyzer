About CipherGuard

CipherGuard is a Password Strength Analyzer that helps users create stronger passwords through real-time analysis, security scoring, entropy calculation, crack time estimation, and personalized suggestions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve cybersecurity awareness by helping users understand what makes a password secure. Through live analysis and educational feedback, CipherGuard encourages better password practices to reduce the risk of cyber threats.
